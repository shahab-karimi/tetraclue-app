Puzzle Trove

Logic puzzle games for iPhone and iPad

Puzzle Trove brings several logic puzzle games together in one app: TOILS (fill every white square with tetrominoes), Color Mosaic (color the mosaic so touching tiles never match) and Nodebound (start on green, visit every node exactly once, finish on yellow), with more on the way. Every puzzle has exactly one solution that you can reach by reasoning.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free? What does Remove Ads do?

Every puzzle in every game is free, with a small banner ad while you play. The one-time Remove Ads purchase turns the ads off in all games for good. It is not a subscription and it doesn't unlock anything else, because everything is already free.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap the gear on the game list to open Settings, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with; this also works on your other iPhone or iPad.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Each game saves its progress and settings automatically on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere, are not synced between devices, and are removed if you delete the app.

Can I change my ad privacy choices?

Where consent is required, Settings (the gear on the game list) shows Ad Privacy Choices. See the privacy policy for details.
