Privacy Policy

Puzzle Trove for iPhone and iPad · Effective October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information the Puzzle Trove app handles, including every game in it (currently TOILS, Color Mosaic and Nodebound). In short: the app has no accounts, the developer does not collect personal information, and your game data stays on your device. Unless you buy Remove Ads, the app shows banner ads provided by Google, which handles some information as described below.

Information stored on your device

Puzzle progress, best times, statistics, hint usage and settings for each game are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. They are not sent to the developer or any server, and they are not synced between your devices. Deleting the app removes them. Like other app data, they may be included in your device backups according to your iCloud or computer backup settings.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is sold through Apple's App Store and removes ads from every game in Puzzle Trove. Apple processes the payment; the app only receives Apple's confirmation of the purchase, which it uses to stop showing ads. The developer does not receive your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Advertising

If you have not bought Remove Ads, a banner ad from Google AdMob is shown while you play a puzzle. There are no ads in How to Play, menus or Settings. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect or receive information such as your IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. Google's use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to Google through Puzzle Trove, and the app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings (the gear on the game list), under Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Ad content is limited to the General Audiences (G) rating.
  • After you buy Remove Ads, the app makes no ad requests.

Information the developer collects

None. Puzzle Trove has no accounts, analytics or crash-reporting services of its own and does not sell or share personal information. If you contact support, the information you include in your request is used only to answer it.

Children

Puzzle Trove is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open a request on the Puzzle Trove support page.
