TOILS:Block Puzzle

Tetromino logic puzzles for iPhone

Each board is a grid of white squares and black obstacles. Fill every white square with tetrominoes, the four-square pieces, so nothing overlaps and nothing is left uncovered. Every puzzle has exactly one solution that you can reach by reasoning.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

The five pieces, T, O, I, L and S, sit in a row below the board. Tap a piece to rotate it and double-tap it to flip it (L also gives J, S also gives Z). Then drag it onto the board and let go. A piece is placed only if all four of its squares land on empty white squares; otherwise the drop is refused and nothing changes. Pieces never run out. Drag a placed piece to move it, or tap it and choose Remove. The puzzle is solved when every white square is covered. Settings > How to Play replays the short tutorial. With VoiceOver, use the Rotate and Flip actions on a piece and the Place action on a board square.

How do hints work?

Tap Hint to see where to look. Tap again to explain the reasoning, then to show the forced piece or an impossible placement. Place for me appears only for forced pieces and only when you ask. Hints come from your current board, so they work in any order. If a piece can't be part of the solution, the hint says so; use Undo, or More > Check Board to mark pieces to review.

What is free and what does the unlock include?

All 634 puzzles at 6×6 and 8×8 are free, with a small banner ad while you play. The one-time Full Unlock purchase adds all 251 puzzles at 10×10, 12×12 and 14×14 and removes every ad in the app, including on 6×6 and 8×8. It is not a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings > Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the unlock screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. The unlock stays available offline.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Progress, best times and settings are saved automatically on your iPhone. They are not uploaded anywhere and are removed if you delete the app.

Can I change my ad privacy choices?

Where consent is required, Settings shows Ad Privacy Choices. See the privacy policy for details.
